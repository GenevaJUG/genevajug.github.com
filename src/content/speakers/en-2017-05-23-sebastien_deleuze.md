Sébastien Deleuze is a Spring Framework and Spring AI committer at Broadcom. He introduced Kotlin support in Spring and works on integrating technologies that improve the efficiency of Spring applications in production, including GraalVM, JVM AOT Cache, and Project Loom. He also works on new ways to build agentic applications with Spring.

He has believed in WebAssembly since 2016 and is a former member of the MiXiT conference staff.
